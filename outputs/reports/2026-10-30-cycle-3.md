## Follow-up (same day): returning-visitor bug + form and button pass

Noah's screenshots after the merge showed an unstyled form, a visible spam-trap field, and a frozen walkthrough. Cause: `netlify.toml` caches `/css/*` and `/js/*` for a day, and the URLs never changed, so returning browsers ran the old `home.css`/`home.js` against the new HTML (the new `home.js` starts the video; the old one never did). A fresh browser rendered the page correctly.

- **Fix:** an Eleventy `v` filter appends a content hash to every CSS/JS URL (`/css/home.css?v=6f68…`), on the homepage and `base.njk`. Every deploy now reaches every browser at once.
- **Form:** the five-field form at the end of the walkthrough is replaced by a two-step dialog (address → name, phone, email, optional situation chips) that every "Free Rental Analysis" button and both address boxes open, so nobody gets scrolled to the bottom of the page. Inline validation, a send-failure fallback, focus handling, a bottom sheet on phones, and the triggering button recorded in `source` and GA4 (`lead_dialog_open`, `lead_step_complete`, `generate_lead.cta`).
- **Handoff:** a compact start card (address box, three proof points, call and email buttons); on phones it sits above the steps.
- **Buttons:** one button box for links and form buttons (they had rendered at different heights), glass ghost buttons, icons on call/email.
- **Polish:** rail dots aligned on the line and labels only where they can't cover content; the custom cursor no longer parks at the top-left corner; the hero fits above the fold at 1280×720; balanced headline wraps; the mobile call bar appears once the hero's address box scrolls away.

Verified with a 153-check headless Chrome suite (six viewports from 1920×1080 to 360×740; every form entry point; validation, send failure, Escape, backdrop, focus trap, keyboard; reduced motion; no-JS fallbacks; rail and button geometry; video scrub; contact page; inner pages), plus static checks on the build (one Netlify form with the same fields as /contact/, versioned assets match content, 455 JSON-LD blocks parse, 0 missing links). Not tested: real iOS Safari and Android hardware.
