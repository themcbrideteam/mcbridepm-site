## Topic Summary

The post covers the complete owner-side process for requiring renters insurance: the legal basis (Georgia DCA Handbook explicitly permits it; O.C.G.A. Title 44, Chapter 7 is silent/permissive), what minimums to set ($100,000 liability floor), how to word the lease clause, how to collect and verify proof (declarations page vs COI, additional interested party vs additional insured), how to monitor for renewals, and what to do when a policy lapses. The post includes a comparison table, an implementation checklist, and 8 FAQs.

Key verified statistics used:
- Georgia renters insurance average: ~$213–$235/year per NerdWallet
- ~45% of U.S. renters carry no coverage per MoneyGeek
- Requiring renters insurance can reduce renter-caused damage exposure by up to 80% per Exactestate/property management industry data
- Georgia DCA Handbook confirms landlord right to require it: https://dca.georgia.gov/node/2886
