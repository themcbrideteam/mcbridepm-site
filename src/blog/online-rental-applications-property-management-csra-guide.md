## Step 3: The Online Application — What Applicants Actually Submit

McBride PM's online application is a digital form hosted inside AppFolio's tenant portal. Each adult (18 or older) who will occupy the property submits a separate application and pays a separate screening fee. Here is what the form collects:

**Personal identification:**
- Full legal name, date of birth, Social Security number
- Government-issued ID (uploaded as a document)
- Current and mailing addresses

**Rental history:**
- Prior addresses for the past two years
- Prior landlord or property manager names and contact information
- Reason for leaving each prior address

**Employment and income:**
- Current employer name, address, and phone number
- Monthly gross income
- Supporting documentation: two most recent paystubs, W-2, tax return, or — for military applicants — an LES (Leave and Earnings Statement), current PCS orders, or a BAH entitlement letter

**Additional applicant data:**
- Emergency contacts
- Vehicle information (for properties with parking restrictions)
- Pets: species, breed, weight, age
- Co-applicants and their relationship to primary applicant

**Consent authorization:**
- Electronic consent to pull a full credit report, run a background check, and verify identity and rental history through TransUnion

The consent form complies with the [Fair Credit Reporting Act (15 U.S.C. §§ 1681 et seq.)](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/statutes/fair-credit-reporting-act), including the required disclosure notice to the applicant before pulling a consumer report. Applicants receive a written summary of their FCRA rights at the time of submission.

**For Fort Gordon military families specifically:** Most property management operations default to "two civilian paystubs" as income documentation — which doesn't map cleanly to how military compensation is structured. McBride PM accepts an LES, current PCS orders, or a BAH entitlement letter as income documentation. Most Fort Gordon E-5-and-above BAH rates for the Columbia County area (Evans ZIP 30809, Grovetown ZIP 30813) at the with-dependent rate comfortably clear our gross income threshold for properties in the $1,500–$1,900 monthly range. The [PCS Landlord Quick-Start Guide](/pdfs/McBride-PM_PCS-Landlord-Quick-Start-Guide.pdf) includes a full breakdown of what military applicants submit and how the income verification maps to BAH rate tables.

## Step 4: What the Screening Actually Checks

Tenant screening is where many self-managed CSRA landlords make expensive mistakes — either using screening tools they haven't set up properly under the FCRA, or asking questions during the application process that create Fair Housing exposure. A professional management relationship removes both risks.

McBride PM runs screening through AppFolio's integrated TransUnion platform. Each application triggers three distinct checks simultaneously:

**Credit report:** A full credit history including score, open accounts, payment history, collections, and derogatory marks. We don't use a single credit score cutoff in isolation — a 590 with a recent eviction is treated differently than a 590 with a few aged medical collections. McBride PM's [published tenant screening criteria](/pdfs/McBride-PM_Tenant-Screening-Standards.pdf) give applicants clear guidance before they submit an application.

**Criminal background:** A national criminal records database search, including registered sex offender registry and global terrorist watchlist checks. Under [HUD's 2016 guidance on the use of criminal records in housing](https://www.hud.gov/press/press_releases_media_advisories/hud_no_16_035), a blanket "no criminal record ever" policy creates Fair Housing exposure by producing disparate impact on protected classes. McBride PM conducts an individualized assessment — offense type, time elapsed, and demonstrated rehabilitation are all factors. This process is detailed further in our [Fair Housing Compliance guide](/blog/fair-housing-compliance-csra-landlords-2026/).

**Eviction history:** A search of eviction filing records across all states. A prior eviction judgment for nonpayment within the last three years is typically disqualifying. A dismissed filing or a lease-violation-based filing from more than five years prior is evaluated in context of the full application.

Results return within 24–48 hours of the screening being ordered. The owner sees the screening outcome in AppFolio — qualified, conditionally qualified, or declined, with the basis category. The full consumer report is not viewable by the owner directly, per FCRA restrictions on how consumer reports can be shared.

This is where the compliance gap in self-management becomes concrete: pulling credit and background checks yourself requires a permissible-purpose relationship with a consumer reporting agency, written consent compliant with FCRA disclosure requirements, and a proper adverse action procedure when you decline. McBride PM's AppFolio integration handles all of this systematically. Owners who DIY this without the proper setup are running FCRA violation risk on every denied application.

## Step 5: Income and Rental History Verification

Screening results tell you what a database report shows. Verification tells you whether what the applicant *submitted* is accurate — two different things.

McBride PM verifies income independently: we call employers directly to confirm employment status and salary, not just accept uploaded paystubs at face value. Paystub fraud is a documented problem in the CSRA rental market; our team flags inconsistencies in font, formatting, and rounding patterns that indicate altered documents. Our [2026 rental fraud guide](/blog/rental-application-fraud-csra-landlords-2026/) covers the most common schemes in detail.

For military applicants, we verify BAH authorization through submitted documents and, when the situation warrants, cross-reference through the Defense Manpower Data Center (DMDC) database.

We also contact prior landlords directly — not a cursory name confirmation, but a structured reference that asks specific questions: Was rent paid on time? Were there lease violations? Were there noise or neighbor complaints? Would you rent to this person again? This step is time-consuming for self-managed owners and often skipped entirely. At McBride PM it is standard on every application.

McBride PM's income threshold is gross monthly income at or above 3× the monthly rent. For a $1,750/month Grovetown single-family home, that means $5,250 gross monthly income. A Fort Gordon E-6 with dependents carrying the Columbia County with-dependent BAH rate for 2026 clears that threshold for most three-bedroom inventory across Evans, Grovetown, and Martinez.

## Step 6: Approval, Denial, and Adverse Action Compliance

Once screening and verification are complete, McBride PM makes the approval or denial decision against our [published screening criteria](/pdfs/McBride-PM_Tenant-Screening-Standards.pdf). Owners see the outcome and reason category in AppFolio within 24–48 hours of the screening completing.

**When an application is approved:** We notify the applicant, move to lease preparation, and collect the first-month security deposit to hold the unit off market. Owners receive a push notification from AppFolio and can see the applicant name, proposed move-in date, and lease terms in the portal. You don't need to call us to find out what's happening — the portal update is the notification.

**When an application is declined:** The applicant receives a written adverse action notice within three business days. This notice is required by [the Fair Credit Reporting Act (15 U.S.C. § 1681m)](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/privacy-security/consumer-reports/using-consumer-reports-landlords-property-managers) and must identify the consumer reporting agency used, state that the decision was based in whole or in part on information from the consumer report, and inform the applicant of their right to obtain a free copy of the report and to dispute its accuracy.

The property owner is not named as the decision-maker in the adverse action notice. This is deliberate. Fair Housing enforcement cases sometimes trace directly to adverse action notices that identify an individual landlord as having made a screening decision. McBride PM's process keeps the decision within our professional compliance layer, not in your name as an individual property owner.

For owners who are out of state and not tracking FCRA compliance requirements — which is most PCS landlords — this is one of the concrete legal protections that a professional management relationship provides that self-management typically doesn't. ## Step 7: From Approval to Executed Digital Lease

When an applicant is approved, McBride PM generates the lease in AppFolio. We use a standard CSRA residential lease that includes all required provisions under Georgia law — key clauses are covered in our [Georgia Lease Agreement Clauses guide](/blog/georgia-lease-agreement-clauses-csra-landlords-2026/).

The approved tenant receives a secure email link to review and e-sign the lease document electronically. No printing, no scanning, no emailed Word documents going back and forth with tracked changes. The signature is timestamped and legally binding under Georgia's Electronic Records and Signatures Act (O.C.G.A. § 10-12-1 et seq.).

When the lease is fully executed, three things happen automatically in AppFolio:

1. The signed PDF is archived in the document center for that unit and accessible from your owner portal indefinitely.
2. You receive a push notification confirming the lease is signed.
3. The unit status changes from "Application Pending" to "Leased," and the move-in date activates on the accounting calendar — your owner statement will begin reflecting the new lease from that date forward.

From a phone in Colorado or Okinawa, you see: unit leased, tenant name, lease start and end date, monthly rent, and a download link for the executed lease. The transaction is complete without a single phone call required from you.

The security deposit is held in McBride PM's trust account per O.C.G.A. § 44-7-31 and tracked separately in AppFolio — it appears on your owner statement as a liability, not as income, until it is either applied to documented damages at move-out or refunded.

## What This Means for CSRA Landlords in Q4 2026

Late September through December is traditionally the slower leasing period in the Augusta market. Back-to-school urgency has passed, and while winter move-ins happen, overall volume is lower than spring or summer. For CSRA landlords with a vacant property or an upcoming lease expiration, two things are worth knowing right now.

**Price is the primary lever.** A property priced above the comparable range for Evans or Martinez will sit longer in Q4 than it would in May or June. As of September 2026, the median rent across all Augusta units is tracking around $1,224 per month according to [Apartment List's September 2026 CSRA report](https://www.apartmentlist.com/rent-report/ga/augusta); Columbia County single-family three-bedrooms are running closer to $1,600–$1,900 depending on ZIP code, condition, and age. If you're at the high end of that range with above-average finishes, the AppFolio-syndicated listing network will give you data quickly — lots of views without applications is a price signal, not a wait-and-see situation.

**Fort Gordon PCS traffic continues year-round.** Unlike the civilian rental market, military-driven moves don't follow a spring-summer calendar. Service members receive assignment orders on 12-month rotation cycles. December and January PCS moves to Fort Gordon are common — incoming personnel from the Army Cyber Center of Excellence, the Signal Corps, and other commands cycle in constantly. A property priced correctly and live on the AppFolio syndication network will be in front of that incoming-assignment audience regardless of the month.

If you have a property coming off lease in November or December, the right time to start the conversation about renewal versus re-listing pricing is now — not when a vacancy is already open.
