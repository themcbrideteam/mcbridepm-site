## How to activate your AppFolio portal and get started

Your AppFolio portal access starts with the invitation email McBride PM sends when your lease begins. If you haven't received one or can't find it, check your spam folder first — invitation emails from property management software sometimes filter there. If it's not there, email robert@mcbride-pm.com and request a new invitation link.

Once you click the link and create a password, you can log in from any browser or download the **AppFolio Online Portal** mobile app, available free for iOS and Android. The app and the browser version have the same core features; most tenants find the app more convenient for quick maintenance photo submissions from their phone camera.

**First things to configure when you log in:**

- **Notification preferences.** Set whether you want email, in-app, or text notifications for maintenance status changes, announcements, and payment confirmations. Turning these on means you hear about your repair schedule without having to check back manually.
- **Emergency contact information.** Verify your phone number is current. During a maintenance emergency — no heat on a cold night, active water leak — McBride PM or the dispatched vendor may call you directly.
- **Renter's profile.** Confirm your unit address and lease term appear correctly. If you notice an error, flag it immediately.

The setup process takes 10 to 15 minutes. If you want to download the McBride PM Tenant Information Form for your own records, it's available at [/resources/](/resources/). That page also holds every PDF we publish for residents.

## Paying rent online: methods, timing, and autopay

The portal's most-used feature is rent payment, and it handles it well — provided you understand a few timing details that catch some tenants off guard.

### What payment methods are accepted

AppFolio's resident portal accepts:

| Method | Processing time | Typical cost |
|--------|-----------------|--------------|
| ACH bank transfer | 1–3 business days | Typically free |
| Credit card | Same day (online) | Processing fee (~2–3%) |
| Debit card | Same day (online) | Processing fee applies |

For most tenants, the ACH option is the right default: no fee and reliable. Card payments are useful for months when you need the float, but the processing fee adds up across a full lease year.

### How to set up autopay

Autopay is the single highest-value change you can make to your rental experience. A late fee costs more time and money than a few minutes of setup.

1. Go to the **Payments** tab in your portal.
2. Select your bank account (or add one under "Add Payment Method").
3. Enable **Recurring Payment** and set the draft date.
4. Set the draft date at least two to three days before your rent due date. If your lease says rent is due on the first, draft on the 28th or 29th of the prior month. That buffer covers weekends and bank processing delays.
5. Confirm the payment amount. If your rent changes at renewal, you'll need to update the autopay amount manually.

Once set, you'll get a confirmation email each month when the payment drafts, and another when it posts. If the payment fails — insufficient funds, expired account — the portal notifies you and you'll have a brief window to correct it before a late fee applies.

### Tenants paying through Section 8 / Housing Choice Vouchers

McBride PM participates in the Housing Choice Voucher program administered through the Augusta Housing Authority. Your tenant contribution — your portion of rent — flows through the portal the same way as a standard lease. The Housing Authority pays its portion directly to McBride PM. You only pay your share through AppFolio. If you ever see a discrepancy in what the portal shows as your balance, call **(706) 339-2874** or email robert@mcbride-pm.com — HCV payments sometimes post on a different timeline than tenant payments.

## Tracking a maintenance request from submission to completion

If you've used the portal to submit a maintenance request, you already know what the submission screen looks like. What most tenants are less clear on is what happens next — specifically, what each status stage means and when it's appropriate to escalate.

For a full guide to writing a well-documented submission — what to include, how to describe an issue precisely, and when to call instead of using the portal — see [The Right Way to Submit a Maintenance Request in Your CSRA Rental](/blog/maintenance-request-tenant-guide-csra-rental/). This section focuses on tracking and follow-up after the request is in.

### The AppFolio work order status stages

After submission, your request moves through these stages in the Maintenance tab:

| Status | What it means | Typical timeframe |
|--------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Received** | McBride PM has the request; it's in the queue | Should move within 1–2 business days |
| **Vendor Contacted** | A licensed vendor has been assigned and reached | 1–3 business days after Received |
| **Scheduled** | Vendor visit date/time is confirmed | You may get a call or portal message with the time window |
| **Completed** | Work is finished | Rate the repair if prompted in the portal |
| **On Hold** | Pending parts, owner approval, or permit | Portal message should explain the specific reason |

You'll receive a notification at each status change if your notification settings are on. If you haven't been getting notifications, go to your profile settings and verify your email address and notification preferences.

### What "On Hold" actually means

"On Hold" generates more tenant questions than any other status. It typically means one of three things:

1. **Parts are on backorder.** HVAC components, plumbing fixtures, and appliance parts sometimes have lead times of several days to a few weeks, depending on supply chain conditions.
2. **An owner decision is required.** For large repairs — full appliance replacement, roof work, structural repairs — McBride PM needs owner authorization before proceeding. This is standard practice and protects both the owner and the tenant from unauthorized work orders.
3. **A permit or inspection is required.** Electrical panel work, gas line repairs, and certain HVAC replacements require a permit from Columbia County, Richmond County, or the relevant municipality. That process adds days to any timeline.

In all three cases, the portal message accompanying the hold status should explain the reason. If it doesn't, call (706) 339-2874 or email robert@mcbride-pm.com and reference the work order number. We can give you an estimated timeline for nearly every hold type.

### When to escalate and what Georgia law requires

Georgia law under [O.C.G.A. § 44-7-13](https://tenant-rights.com/georgia/georgia-ocga-44-7-13-landlord-repair-duty-guide) requires landlords to maintain rental properties in good repair for the duration of the tenancy. The [Georgia Safe at Home Act](https://gaappleseed.org/wp-content/uploads/Safe-at-Home-Act.pdf), effective July 1, 2024, added a statutory warranty of habitability — residential leases are treated as including a promise that the unit is fit for human habitation, and that duty cannot be waived by lease language.

For your practical purposes, the response-time framework works like this:

- **Emergencies (24–48 hours):** No heat when outdoor temperatures are below 50°F, no running water, gas or carbon monoxide leak, active flooding, broken exterior door lock. Don't use the portal for emergencies — call **(706) 339-2874** immediately.
- **Urgent (7–14 days):** Appliance failure, HVAC inefficiency, pest activity, plumbing leak without active flooding.
- **Routine (14–30 days):** Wear items, cosmetic issues, minor fixture malfunctions that don't affect habitability.

If an urgent issue is sitting at "Received" for more than two business days without a status update, call **(706) 339-2874** directly. Have the submission date and request description ready. McBride PM tracks every open work order; there's a resolution path for every stuck ticket.

For a full explanation of your rights when a repair is delayed or denied, see the [Georgia Tenant Rights 2026 Guide for CSRA Renters](/blog/georgia-tenant-rights-2026-csra-renter-guide/).

*This is general guidance from a property manager — not legal advice. Talk to a Georgia-licensed attorney for your specific situation.*

## Communicating with your property manager through the portal

The portal's messaging feature creates a written record of your conversations with McBride PM — which benefits you in several practical ways. Oral conversations are hard to verify months later; portal messages have timestamps. If a maintenance issue or a charge becomes a dispute, your documented communication is already there.

Use the portal messaging for:

- Asking non-urgent questions about your lease or unit
- Requesting clarification on a charge on your account statement
- Notifying us of a change in your access schedule for an upcoming maintenance visit
- Following up on a work order that hasn't progressed

Use the phone for:

- Emergencies (always phone first: **(706) 339-2874**)
- Time-sensitive scheduling changes the same day as a vendor visit
- Anything that needs a same-day response

McBride PM monitors portal messages during business hours. AppFolio's system also supports [AI-assisted message handling](https://www.appfolio.com/blog/best-tools-for-tenant-communication-2026) that routes and triages incoming messages — but our team reviews every message before acting on it. If your question needs a human response, it gets one.

One practical note: when you're following up on a maintenance request through messaging, reference the specific request (by date submitted or description). That gets your message to the right work order faster than a general "any update?" message.

## Viewing and downloading your lease documents

One of the most useful but least-used portal functions is the document library. Under the **Documents** or **Leases** tab, you'll find:

- Your signed lease agreement
- Any addenda (pet addendum, mold addendum, move-in acknowledgment, renewal terms)
- Your move-in inspection report
- Notices McBride PM has sent you during your tenancy
- Your current statement of account

You can download any of these as a PDF. That matters more than it might seem:

- **Rental references.** When you apply for a future rental, a prospective landlord may request proof of your current lease. You can pull it yourself in under two minutes.
- **Background checks and employment verifications.** Some employers and lenders ask for residential address verification going back two years. Your lease PDF covers it.
- **Lease term disputes.** If there's ever a disagreement about what your lease says — grace periods, pet policy details, notice requirements — you have the executed document on hand.

If your move-in inspection report isn't showing in the portal, contact us to request it. You want that document well before move-out — it's the baseline record against which your unit's condition at move-out is assessed, and it directly affects your security deposit. The full guide on protecting your deposit is at [How to Move Out of a CSRA Rental Without Losing Your Deposit](/blog/how-to-move-out-csra-rental-keep-security-deposit/).

## Renter's insurance: what the portal handles and what you track

Some lease agreements require tenants to maintain a renter's insurance policy and list the property owner as an additional interested party. If yours does, you may be able to upload proof of coverage directly in the portal's documents section.

What the portal won't do automatically: verify your coverage or remind you when your policy lapses. That's your responsibility to track.

Renter's insurance for a typical CSRA rental — Evans, Grovetown, Martinez, Augusta, or the Richmond County corridor — runs roughly $15 to $25 per month for $30,000 in personal property coverage and $100,000 in liability. It covers your belongings in fire, theft, and certain water damage scenarios. It does NOT cover the building structure (that's the owner's landlord policy). The full breakdown of what's covered and what isn't is in our [Renter's Insurance Georgia Guide](/blog/renters-insurance-georgia-what-it-covers/).

If your lease requires renter's insurance and you let your policy lapse, that's a lease violation. The portal document upload makes it easy to stay current and give us the updated declaration page.

## Fall maintenance to request through the portal right now

If you're reading this in late October or early November, there's a practical list of portal submissions worth making before temperatures drop across the CSRA. These are non-emergencies, which means they're processed through the normal work order queue — getting them in now, before the seasonal rush of heating calls that arrives every December, typically means faster resolution.

**Submit a portal request for any of these before the end of November:**

1. **Heating system function test.** Switch your thermostat to heat and run it for 15 to 20 minutes. If it doesn't reach temperature, makes unusual sounds, or smells like something burning beyond the first cycle of dust burning off, submit a request. An HVAC technician can catch a failing heat exchanger or a dirty filter now, not on the coldest night of January when every vendor in Columbia County is booked.

2. **Window or door drafts.** Run your hand around the perimeter of exterior-facing doors and windows on a windy day. A noticeable cold air draft means weatherstripping may need replacement. This is a routine work order, but it's also a habitability item — your unit should be able to retain heat.

3. **Water heater sediment.** If you hear popping or rumbling from your water heater (typically in a utility closet or garage), that's mineral sediment buildup from the CSRA's water chemistry. It's not an emergency, but it's a sign the unit needs a flush. Older water heaters — anything over 10 to 12 years — may already be on a replacement watch list. Submit a note through the portal and let us verify the age on file.

4. **Ceiling or wall stains from summer humidity.** Now that fall's lower humidity makes them visible, small water stains from summer roof drainage or condensation are worth documenting. They may be old and dry (cosmetic only), or they may indicate a slow leak that worsens through winter. Either way, you want them documented and assessed before January.

5. **Pest activity around entry points.** Fall is prime time for mice and insects to seek warmth through any gap in a foundation or exterior wall. Georgia law under O.C.G.A. § 44-7-13 places pest control responsibility on the landlord where infestation wasn't caused by tenant conduct. An early submission — first signs of mouse activity, roaches near an exterior wall, ant trails entering from outside — gets ahead of an infestation that's far more expensive to remediate in winter.

For the full McBride PM HVAC maintenance framework — what we handle, what vendors we use, and what to expect in response times — see [HVAC Guide for CSRA Rental Property Owners and Tenants](/blog/hvac-csra-rental-property-landlord-guide-augusta-ga/). The [Maintenance Request Guide PDF](/pdfs/McBride-PM_Maintenance-Request-Guide.pdf) summarizes the full scope of what to submit, how to document it, and what response windows look like by issue type.

## Giving move-out notice through the portal

When you're ready to move out, your lease almost certainly requires written notice at least 30 days before your move-out date — confirm your specific lease terms, as some leases require 60 days. The portal's messaging feature creates a documented written record that's timestamped and tied to your account.

Send your notice to vacate through portal messaging AND follow up by downloading the [30-Day Notice to Vacate PDF](/pdfs/McBride-PM_30-Day-Notice-to-Vacate.pdf) from [/resources/](/resources/) and emailing it to robert@mcbride-pm.com. Using both channels eliminates any ambiguity about when notice was given and received.

If you're breaking your lease before the end of the term, the portal doesn't handle that negotiation — the process, your options, and the financial implications under Georgia law are covered in detail in [Breaking a Lease Early in Georgia: A CSRA Tenant's Guide](/blog/breaking-lease-early-georgia-csra-tenant-guide/).

Before your final walkthrough, download the [Move-Out Inspection Checklist PDF](/pdfs/McBride-PM_Move-Out-Inspection-Checklist.pdf) and compare it against your move-in inspection report in the portal. The condition difference between those two documents — accounting for normal wear and tear — is the basis for your security deposit disposition. If you haven't accessed your move-in inspection report in the portal yet, do it before your move-out date, not after.

## Portal troubleshooting: common issues and fixes

[AppFolio's resident portal](https://www.appfolio.com/property-manager/communication-service/resident-experience) is reliable for daily use across our portfolio of 240+ rental homes in Evans, Grovetown, Martinez, Augusta, and the broader CSRA. Like any web application, it occasionally has hiccups.

**Common issues and what to try:**

- **Login loops or authentication failures.** Clear your browser cache, or try the mobile app instead of a browser. If neither works, use the "Forgot Password" flow to reset your credentials.
- **Payment not showing as posted.** ACH payments take one to three business days to process. A payment submitted on Friday won't post until Monday or Tuesday. If it's been more than three business days, contact us.
- **No status updates on a maintenance request.** Check your notification settings — they may be turned off. Also check portal messages for a note McBride PM may have sent about the request.
- **Documents tab appears empty.** This sometimes happens if your tenant record hasn't fully synced after a lease renewal or a new move-in. Email robert@mcbride-pm.com and we'll get the documents attached to your account.
- **Wrong rent amount in portal.** If the portal shows a different balance than your lease specifies, contact us before your payment due date. Don't overpay or underpay while waiting to sort it out — call (706) 339-2874 or email robert@mcbride-pm.com and we'll reconcile it.
