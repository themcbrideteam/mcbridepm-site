## Why Martinez Keeps Showing Up on Renters' Shortlists

If you've been searching for rentals in the Augusta area, you've probably noticed Martinez popping up more and more. There's a reason for that. Sitting between downtown Augusta and the newer developments in Evans, Martinez gives renters something that's increasingly hard to find: a central location with reasonable rents and established infrastructure.

Martinez isn't the flashy new-construction suburb. It's the neighborhood that's been quietly delivering for decades — mature tree-lined streets, grocery stores within a five-minute drive, and the kind of neighborhood stability that keeps tenants renewing year after year. At McBride Property Management, we manage properties across Columbia County, and Martinez consistently ranks among the areas with the strongest tenant satisfaction.

## Where Exactly Is Martinez?

Martinez is an unincorporated community in western Columbia County, bordered roughly by Wheeler Road to the south, Fury's Ferry Road to the north, the Savannah River to the east, and Evans to the west. It's not a city with its own government — it falls under Columbia County jurisdiction — but it has a distinct identity and ZIP code (30907).

For renters, this matters because you get Columbia County services (fire, water, roads, schools) without a separate city tax layer. Your landlord's lower tax burden often translates to more competitive rents compared to properties inside Augusta city limits.

### Key landmarks and reference points

Martinez is anchored by a few corridors that renters should know. **Washington Road (GA-28)** runs through the heart of the community and connects directly to downtown Augusta in about 15 minutes. **Columbia Road** feeds south toward Bobby Jones Expressway, and **Baston Road** connects to Evans and the western side of the county. If you work at [Fort Gordon](/blog/fort-eisenhower-military-tenant-rental-demand-augusta/), you're looking at a 20-to-25-minute commute depending on which gate you use.

## What Renters Can Expect to Pay

Rental pricing in Martinez tends to run slightly below Evans but above most Augusta city neighborhoods. As of early 2026, you can generally expect the following ranges for Columbia County's Martinez area:

- **Single-family homes (3BR/2BA):** $1,300–$1,700/month depending on age, condition, and proximity to Washington Road
- **Townhomes and duplexes:** $1,100–$1,400/month
- **Apartments:** $950–$1,250/month for newer complexes

## Neighborhood Breakdown: Where to Focus Your Search

### West Martinez (Near Evans Border)

This area along Baston Road and William Few Parkway offers newer construction and proximity to Evans' shopping and dining. Subdivisions like **Summerwood** and **Westfield** feature homes built in the 2000s with updated floor plans. Rents trend toward the higher end of the Martinez range here, but you're also closer to Riverwatch Parkway retail and the new medical offices along Washington Road.

### Central Martinez (Washington Road Corridor)

The core of Martinez — established neighborhoods with homes built from the 1970s through the 1990s. Areas around **Woodhaven**, **National Hills**, and **Stonington** offer larger lots, mature landscaping, and rents that are often $100–$200 below comparable properties in Evans. You're also walking distance to shopping centers, restaurants, and the Columbia County Library's main branch.

### North Martinez (Fury's Ferry Area)

The stretch along Fury's Ferry Road toward the river is quieter and more rural-feeling, with larger properties and more space between neighbors. If you're looking for a single-family rental with a big yard and don't mind being a few minutes further from the commercial corridors, this area delivers. Amber McBride, property manager at McBride Property Management, often recommends this area for families with pets or tenants who want outdoor space without the premium that newer Evans subdivisions command.

## Schools in the Martinez Area

Columbia County consistently ranks among the top school systems in Georgia, and Martinez falls within several well-regarded school zones. Renters with children should know that your rental address determines your school assignment — this is true countywide.

Martinez-area rentals are commonly zoned for schools along the Washington Road and Baston Road corridors. If school assignments are a priority for your family, confirm the specific zoning for any property before signing a lease. ## Getting Around from Martinez

One of Martinez's biggest draws for renters is its central position in the CSRA. Here's what typical commutes look like:

- **Downtown Augusta:** 12–18 minutes via Washington Road or Bobby Jones Expressway
- **Fort Gordon (Gate 1):** 20–25 minutes via Gordon Highway
- **Augusta University / Medical District:** 10–15 minutes
- **Evans Town Center:** 8–12 minutes
- **Augusta Regional Airport:** 25–30 minutes

Public transit options are limited — Augusta Transit has some routes along Washington Road, but most Martinez residents rely on personal vehicles. If you're PCS-ing to Fort Gordon, the commute from Martinez is manageable and avoids the congestion that some Augusta corridors see during peak hours.

## What Makes Martinez Different from Evans or Augusta

Renters often compare Martinez to its neighbors, and the differences matter.

**vs. Evans:** Evans is Columbia County's growth engine — newer construction, more retail, higher rents. Martinez offers similar school quality and county services at a lower price point, with the trade-off being older housing stock in some areas. If you want to be in Columbia County but don't need the newest build, Martinez is where your dollar stretches further.

**vs. Augusta (Richmond County):** Moving into Augusta city limits means a different school system, different tax structure, and generally lower rents — but also different service levels. Martinez gives you Columbia County's infrastructure and school system while keeping you minutes from Augusta's job centers and entertainment.
