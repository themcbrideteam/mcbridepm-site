Full-Service Property Management in Augusta, GA

Comprehensive property management solutions designed to maximize your investment returns while minimizing your stress. We handle every detail so you can focus on growth.

At McBride Property Management, we take pride in managing every aspect of your rental property. From day one, our experienced team handles the responsibilities that typically consume a property owner's time—marketing vacant units, screening tenants, collecting rent, coordinating maintenance, managing finances, and ensuring full legal compliance. Whether you own a single-family home, a multi-family complex, or a commercial property, we provide customized solutions to fit your needs.

Serving property owners throughout the CSRA (Central Savannah River Area), including Augusta, Evans, Grovetown, Martinez, and Columbia County, we've built our reputation on reliable service, transparent communication, and a deep understanding of Georgia and South Carolina real estate laws. Let us help you transform property ownership from a burden into a genuine investment.

Property Marketing & Leasing

Getting your vacant property leased quickly is crucial to your bottom line. We employ a proven marketing strategy that fills vacancies in an average of 14 days, significantly faster than the Augusta area average.

Our approach includes professional photography that showcases your property in the best light, strategically placed listings on major platforms including Zillow, Realtor.com, Apartments.com, and other regional marketplaces. We also combine digital reach with old-fashioned door knocking and local outreach to ensure your property attracts qualified tenants. Our marketing efforts are designed to generate multiple qualified leads and secure the best possible tenant fit at competitive market rates.

Tenant Screening

We protect your investment by thoroughly vetting every applicant using a comprehensive five-criteria evaluation process. Each prospective tenant undergoes credit checks, income verification, rental history review, criminal background screening, and eviction history searches.

Our goal is to identify reliable tenants who will pay rent on time, care for your property, and minimize vacancies and damage. We provide a complete tenant guarantee that covers non-payment of rent, protecting your income stream. Every screening decision is made in compliance with Fair Housing laws to ensure fair treatment for all applicants.

Rent Collection

Rent collection is automated and convenient for your tenants with our online payment portal. Tenants can pay via bank transfer, credit card, or check—all tracked in one easy-to-manage system. We maintain an impressive 99% on-time payment rate, meaning consistent, reliable cash flow for your investment.

Late payments are rare because our system sends automated reminders and our team enforces late fees consistently. In the unfortunate event that a tenant fails to pay, we have a proven eviction process in place to resolve the situation quickly and legally. You'll never have to chase down overdue rent.

Maintenance & Repairs

A well-maintained property is a valuable property. We provide 24/7 emergency response for urgent issues—burst pipes, heating failures, or electrical problems don't wait for business hours, and neither do we. For routine maintenance, we manage a vetted network of trusted contractors and vendors who deliver quality work at fair prices.

Our proactive inspection schedule catches issues before they become expensive problems. Tenants can submit maintenance requests through our online portal, ensuring transparent communication and quick resolution. Preventive maintenance extends property life, reduces costly emergency repairs, and keeps tenants satisfied and retention high.

Financial Reporting

Understanding your property's financial performance is essential for smart ownership decisions. You receive detailed monthly statements showing all income and expenses, rent received, maintenance costs, and our management fees. At year-end, we prepare comprehensive tax documentation that your accountant will appreciate—all income and deductible expenses are clearly categorized and organized.

Access your property's finances anytime through our 24/7 owner portal. Review transactions, download statements, and track your investment performance in real time. Complete financial transparency and easy access mean you always know exactly how your property is performing and where your money is going.

Lease Management & Compliance

Real estate law is complex and varies between Georgia and South Carolina. We create legally sound lease agreements that protect your interests while complying with all applicable federal, state, and local regulations. Our leases are reviewed regularly to ensure they reflect current laws and best practices.

From lease signing through renewal or termination, we manage the entire process. We handle lease renewals, coordinate move-out inspections, process security deposit returns, and manage the eviction process if necessary—all in full compliance with Fair Housing laws and state regulations. This comprehensive legal management protects you from costly disputes and ensures every interaction with tenants follows proper procedure.
