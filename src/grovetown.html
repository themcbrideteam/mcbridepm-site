Grovetown Rental Market Snapshot

Grovetown is the fastest-growing city in Columbia County and one of the fastest-growing in Georgia. New-construction subdivisions have multiplied along the Wrightsboro Road and Lewiston Road corridors over the past decade, driven by Fort Gordon's cyber-mission expansion and the broader Augusta metro's job growth.

  • Typical single-family rent: $1,400–$2,100/month for newer (2015+) construction; $1,200–$1,650/month for established homes.
  • 4-bedroom homes: $1,800–$2,400/month in well-zoned subdivisions.
  • Tenant mix: heavily weighted toward Fort Gordon military families, government contractors, and young professional families. Grovetown is the highest-volume military rental submarket in the CSRA outside the city of Augusta itself.
  • Days on market: 14 days portfolio average. Newer construction with strong school zoning typically leases in under 10.

Who Rents in Grovetown — and What That Means for Owners

Grovetown's tenant base is the most military-heavy in our portfolio. That has specific implications:

  • Price to BAH. Many of your applicants are PCS-ing in with a fixed Basic Allowance for Housing. Pricing your home at or slightly under the local BAH rate for that grade is the single fastest way to fill it.
  • Expect 2–3 year tenancies. Military tours are typically 2–3 years. Plan for turnover on a predictable schedule and budget for make-ready accordingly.
  • Be flexible on application timing. Military families often apply sight-unseen or with very short notice. A property manager who can accommodate virtual showings and quick decisions wins these applicants.
  • Understand the SCRA. The Servicemembers Civil Relief Act gives active-duty tenants specific lease-break rights on receipt of PCS orders. Your lease needs to handle this correctly.

If you're considering buying in Grovetown specifically for the military rental demand, our Fort Gordon rental demand guide walks through how to think about it.

Schools, Commute, and Quality-of-Life Factors

Grovetown is part of the Columbia County School District. The newer subdivisions in particular feed into highly-rated elementary and middle schools, which makes them magnetic for families. Properties zoned for Greenbrier High and the newer Grovetown High typically command stronger rent than otherwise-comparable homes.

Commute is the killer feature: Grovetown sits a 10–20-minute drive from Fort Gordon Gate 1 (Gordon Highway), depending on traffic. That's noticeably faster than Evans or Martinez for personnel commuting to the cyber side of post. I-20 access via Lewiston Road also opens up the broader Augusta metro and even Aiken.

Newer construction is a real differentiator in Grovetown more than in most Columbia County markets — tenants here strongly prefer modern finishes, open floor plans, and energy-efficient HVAC, and they'll pay for it.
