How to Apply for a Rental Property

Browse Available Properties

Explore our current listings above. Each property shows the address, bedrooms, bathrooms, square footage, and monthly rent. Click "Apply Now" on any property that interests you.

Submit Your Application

Complete our secure online application through the AppFolio portal. Provide your personal information, employment details, rental history, and references. You'll pay a non-refundable application fee (typically $35-$50) at this time.

Screening Review

Our leasing team reviews your application, runs a background check, verifies employment, and contacts previous landlords. This process typically takes 1-3 business days. We'll contact you with the results and next steps.

Lease Signing and Move-In

If approved, we'll schedule a lease signing appointment. You'll provide the security deposit and first month's rent, receive the keys, and move in. Our team will coordinate a final walkthrough and answer any questions.
