How to Apply for a Rental Property

Browse and Schedule a Showing

Explore our current listings above. Each home shows the address, bedrooms, bathrooms, square footage, and monthly rent. Pick a showing time that works for you. Before a self-guided tour, you'll verify your identity with a photo of your ID and a quick selfie.

Submit Your Application

Click "Apply Now" on the home you want and complete our secure online application. You'll provide your personal information, income, and rental history, and you'll see the non-refundable application fee before you pay it.

Screening Review

Our leasing team reviews your application, including your credit, background, income, and rental history. This process typically takes 1-3 business days. We'll contact you with the results and next steps.

Lease Signing and Move-In

If approved, we'll schedule a lease signing appointment. You'll provide the security deposit and first month's rent, receive the keys, and move in. Our team will coordinate a final walkthrough and answer any questions.
