North Augusta Rental Market Snapshot

North Augusta sits on the South Carolina side of the Savannah River, directly across from downtown Augusta. The combination of an established quality-of-life community, lower SC property tax rates, and direct access to Augusta's employment centers makes it one of the most resilient rental submarkets in the metro.

  • Typical single-family rent: $1,300–$2,000/month, with newer Riverside Village area homes and well-maintained historic downtown properties at the upper end.
  • Townhomes & smaller homes: $1,100–$1,500/month.
  • Tenant mix: medical professionals working in Augusta, government and Savannah River Site contractors, military families who prefer SC tax treatment, and remote-work professionals attracted to the Riverside Village amenities.
  • Why SC matters: South Carolina has lower property tax rates than Georgia for owner-occupied homes, and the state has a different landlord-tenant statute than Georgia. McBride PM is licensed in both states — here's a refresher on SC landlord-tenant law.

Who Rents in North Augusta

North Augusta's rental demographic is distinct from Augusta's. The most common profiles:

  • Medical professionals at Augusta-area hospitals who want to live in SC for the tax difference
  • Government and Savannah River Site contractors commuting south to SRS via I-520
  • Military families and Fort Gordon contractors who prefer SC's tax treatment over Georgia's
  • Established professionals attracted to the Riverside Village amenities and Greeneway trail lifestyle
  • USC Aiken faculty and graduate students who want shorter commutes than Aiken proper

The takeaway for owners: North Augusta tenants are typically more established, longer-tenured, and willing to pay for finish quality and location. Underpricing leaves real money on the table.

Schools, Commute, and Quality-of-Life Factors

North Augusta is in the Aiken County Public School District. Properties zoned for North Augusta Elementary, Paul Knox Middle, and North Augusta High all draw family rental demand. School zoning matters meaningfully in this market — tenants doing their research will filter by it.

Commute is North Augusta's secret weapon. Most North Augusta locations are 10–20 minutes to downtown Augusta, 15–25 to the medical corridor, and 25–40 to either Fort Gordon or Savannah River Site via I-520. That makes it a viable home base for almost any major CSRA employer.

Quality-of-life amenities — Riverside Village, SRP Park (home of the Augusta GreenJackets), the Greeneway trail, and the historic downtown — drive a real premium for properties within walking or short-driving distance.
