Martinez Rental Market Snapshot

Martinez is one of the most underrated rental submarkets in Columbia County. It doesn't have the buzzy new construction of Evans or the explosive growth of Grovetown, but it has something investors should care about more: stable, established neighborhoods with mature tenant pools that don't churn.

  • Typical single-family rent: $1,300–$1,950/month, depending on size, condition, and school zone.
  • Townhomes & condos: $1,150–$1,500/month, particularly in the Belair Road and West Lake corridors.
  • Tenant mix: long-tenured Augusta-area professionals, medical staff, military families looking for slightly more space than Evans for the money, and families prioritizing the Columbia County school district.
  • Tenant retention: Martinez retention rates are very strong — mature subdivisions self-select for renters who plan to stay.

Martinez Neighborhoods We Manage

West Lake & Bartram Trail corridor

Established subdivisions with strong school zoning. Larger lots and mature landscaping. Tenants here are typically families looking for room to grow without the price tag of Evans Town Center.

Belair Road / Old Petersburg corridor

A mix of older custom homes and 1990s–2000s subdivisions. Convenient to both Washington Road and I-20, with quick commutes to Fort Eisenhower and the Augusta medical corridor.

Bath / Furys Ferry area

Quieter, more residential pockets with established tree canopy. Popular with medical professionals and remote workers who want a quieter setting without giving up the commute.

Augusta Country Club / Walton Way Extension

Higher-end Martinez. Larger homes, established neighborhoods, and tenants in the $2,000–$2,800/month range — typically attendings, senior contractors, and dual-income professional couples.

Who Rents in Martinez

Martinez tenants are typically more established than tenants in newer-construction markets like Grovetown. They're often:

  • Families with school-aged kids who want the Columbia County school district at a slightly lower price point than Evans
  • Medical professionals working in the Augusta corridor who want a 15–20 minute commute
  • Military families (officers and senior NCOs) who've been in the area before and chose Martinez over Evans for the price-to-space ratio
  • Long-term renters who've lived in the same property for 3+ years — this is more common in Martinez than almost anywhere else in our portfolio

The implication: invest in your long-term tenants. Renewals at modest increases are the highest-ROI strategy in this market.

Schools, Commute, and Quality-of-Life Factors

Martinez is part of the Columbia County School District, which is the same draw as Evans — just with slightly less competition for inventory in the rental market. Properties zoned for Lakeside, Greenbrier, and Stallings Island feeders consistently command the strongest rent and longest tenant retention.

Commute is the second factor. Martinez sits roughly equidistant from Fort Eisenhower (20–30 minutes) and downtown Augusta (15–20 minutes), making it a natural fit for tenants whose work splits between the two. Easy access to Washington Road, I-20, and Furys Ferry Road keeps drive times predictable.

Quality-of-life amenities matter too — Savannah Rapids Park, the Augusta Canal trail, and the Bartram Trail school complex all show up regularly in tenant searches.
