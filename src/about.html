About McBride Property Management

Locally owned and operated in Evans, Georgia—no franchises, no call centers, just dedicated expertise.

McBride Property Management was founded on a simple principle: property owners deserve a local partner who knows the market, understands the challenges, and stands behind their commitments. Based in Evans, Georgia, we're not a franchise operation or remote call center. We're a locally owned company with deep roots in the Augusta real estate community.

Led by Broker Noah McBride, our team brings over 50 years of combined experience in Augusta and Aiken real estate. Our family-focused approach means you're working with people who genuinely care about your investment's success—not a generic corporate entity.

Our Approach

Communication

Proactive updates and market reports—you'll hear from us regularly, not just when something goes wrong. Transparency is how we build trust.

Systems

AppFolio platform, online portals, digital lease signing, and automated rent collection. Modern tools make management simple and secure.

Accountability

No long-term contracts required. We earn your business every month through transparent reporting and consistent results.
