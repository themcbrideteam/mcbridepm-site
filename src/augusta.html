Augusta Rental Market Snapshot

Augusta is the second-largest metro in Georgia and the population center of Richmond County. The market is shaped by three big anchors that don't go away in a downturn: an Army installation, a major medical complex, and a growing cyber-and-tech employer base. That gives single-family rental owners a relatively stable tenant pipeline year-round.

  • Typical single-family rent: $1,200–$2,000/month, with newer construction and homes in the West Augusta / National Hills corridor commanding the upper end.
  • Townhomes & smaller homes: $1,000–$1,400/month is common.
  • Average days on market (our portfolio): 14 days, well below the Augusta metro average.
  • Tenant mix: Active-duty military and contractors tied to Fort Gordon, medical staff working in the Augusta University Medical Center / Wellstar MCG corridor, university faculty and staff, and a growing remote-worker contingent.
  • Seasonal pattern: Strongest leasing demand runs roughly March through August, with a secondary bump around the December PCS cycle.

Augusta Neighborhoods We Manage

Augusta is geographically large, and submarkets behave very differently. Here's how we think about the rental landscape across the city:

West Augusta / National Hills

Established residential neighborhoods convenient to Augusta National, Daniel Field, and the Washington Road retail corridor. Tenants here skew toward established professionals and medical staff. Rents tend to be in the $1,500–$2,300 range for single-family homes.

Summerville & the Hill

Historic Augusta with mature trees and walkability to Augusta University. Strong demand from university faculty, residents, and graduate students. Older homes here require an owner who's comfortable budgeting for proactive maintenance.

South Augusta & Tobacco Road Corridor

More affordable price points, often $950–$1,400/month for single-family homes. Heavy demand from Fort Gordon personnel because of the short commute via Gordon Highway. Section 8 / Housing Choice Voucher properties perform well here.

East Augusta & Downtown

Revitalized downtown is attracting younger professionals working in the cyber sector and at the riverfront medical campuses. Loft conversions and townhomes are the dominant rental product. Pricing is highly condition- and amenity-dependent.

Forest Hills & National Hills Subdivisions

Family-oriented neighborhoods with good Richmond County school zones. Strong long-term tenant retention, which is exactly what you want as a rental owner — every renewal you don't have to market is roughly two weeks of vacancy you don't have to absorb.

Who Rents in Augusta — and What That Means for Owners

Knowing who your likely tenant pool is changes how you price, market, and screen. In Augusta we see four dominant tenant profiles:

  • Military families assigned to Fort Gordon: Typically 2–3-year tours, BAH-funded, and culturally strong renters. The Basic Allowance for Housing schedule sets a clear ceiling on what they can pay, which is useful for pricing. Many arrive on short notice (PCS) and need quick move-ins.
  • Medical professionals: Residents and fellows at Wellstar MCG / Augusta University Medical Center cycle on roughly one-year academic calendars (often starting in late June). Attendings and nurses are typically longer-term renters in the $1,500–$2,500 range.
  • University staff and graduate students: Augusta University, Paine College, and the Medical College of Georgia drive demand near Summerville and downtown.
  • Cyber, government, and contractor employees: Augusta's growing cyber sector (anchored by Fort Gordon's cyber mission) is producing a steady stream of professional renters earning $75K+ who want move-in-ready properties.

Our tenant screening process is calibrated for this mix — including specific handling for BAH-backed military tenants, Section 8 voucher holders, and university-cycle leases.

Schools, Commute, and Quality-of-Life Factors

Tenant decisions in Augusta are heavily driven by three things: school district, distance to Fort Gordon or the medical corridor, and access to the I-20 / Bobby Jones Expressway loop.

Richmond County School District anchors most family rentals; properties zoned for higher-rated elementary schools (especially in West Augusta and parts of South Augusta) consistently rent faster and at higher price points than otherwise-similar homes a few blocks away. If you're considering buying an Augusta rental, the school zoning matters as much as the bedroom count.

For commute, anything within 15 minutes of Fort Gordon's Gate 1 (Gordon Highway and 15th Street) has a deeper military tenant pool. Anything within 10 minutes of the Wellstar MCG / Augusta University Medical campus draws medical tenants who'd rather pay a premium than fight rush-hour traffic on Walton Way or 15th Street.
