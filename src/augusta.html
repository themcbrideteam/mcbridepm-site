Augusta Rental Market Snapshot

Augusta is the second-largest metro in Georgia and the population center of Richmond County. The market is shaped by three big anchors that don't go away in a downturn: an Army installation, a major medical complex, and a growing cyber-and-tech employer base. That gives single-family rental owners a relatively stable tenant pipeline year-round.

  • Typical single-family rent: $1,200–$2,000/month, with newer construction and homes in the West Augusta / National Hills corridor commanding the upper end.
  • Townhomes & smaller homes: $1,000–$1,400/month is common.
  • Average days on market (our portfolio): 14 days, well below the Augusta metro average.
  • Tenant mix: active-duty military and contractors tied to Fort Gordon, medical staff in the Augusta University Medical Center / Wellstar MCG corridor, university faculty and staff, and a growing remote-worker contingent.
  • Seasonal pattern: strongest leasing demand runs roughly March through August, with a secondary bump around the December PCS cycle.

Neighborhoods We Serve in Augusta

Augusta is geographically large, and its submarkets behave very differently. Our local knowledge runs deep across the city's most sought-after residential areas:

  • Summerville & the Hill — historic Augusta with mature trees and walkability to Augusta University; strong demand from faculty, residents, and graduate students.
  • West Augusta — established neighborhoods near Augusta National and the Washington Road corridor; professionals and medical staff, with rents at the upper end of the market.
  • National Hills — family-oriented subdivisions with good Richmond County school zones and strong long-term tenant retention.
  • Forest Hills — established, family-friendly streets near the historic course; reliable, long-tenured renters.
  • The Medical District corridor — high demand from travel nurses, residents, and hospital staff who want a short commute to the hospitals.
